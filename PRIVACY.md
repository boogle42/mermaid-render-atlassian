# Privacy policy

Mermaid Render for Jira, Confluence & Bitbucket does not collect, store or transmit any personal data or page content.

- Diagrams are drawn entirely in your browser by a copy of Mermaid bundled with the extension.
- The extension makes no network requests.
- **Open in Mermaid Live** is the one exception, and only when you click it. It opens mermaid.live, a third-party site, in a new tab with a copy of that diagram's source. The source is in the link's fragment (after `#`), which browsers don't send to the server; Mermaid Live then reads it in your browser, under its own privacy policy. You can hide the link in the extension's settings.
- It reads code blocks only on the sites listed in its settings, and only to decide whether they contain Mermaid and to draw them.
- Your settings (diagram theme, whether the Mermaid Live link shows, and any extra sites you add) are saved with Chrome's sync storage, which Chrome syncs between your own signed-in browsers. They are not sent anywhere else.
