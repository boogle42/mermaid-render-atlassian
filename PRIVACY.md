# Privacy policy

Mermaid Render for Jira, Confluence & Bitbucket does not collect, store or transmit any personal data or page content.

- Diagrams are drawn entirely in your browser by a copy of Mermaid bundled with the extension.
- The extension makes no network requests.
- It reads code blocks only on the sites listed in its settings, and only to decide whether they contain Mermaid and to draw them.
- Your settings (diagram theme and any extra sites you add) are saved with Chrome's sync storage, which Chrome syncs between your own signed-in browsers. They are not sent anywhere else.
